Features

  • Mobile charger designed specifically for use in vehicles and trailers
  • Switch between 6 A daytime-charging and 2.5 A overnight-charging mode for circuit maximisation
  • Charge up to 72 M18™ batteries on a single outlet with the overnight-charging-mode with 12 chargers
  • 6 M18™ bays
  • REDLINK™ Intelligence: Optimises charge rates based on the battery type and temperature to minimise charge duration and maximise battery lifespan
  • Convenient and adjustable carrying handle
  • Charges 6x M18 5 Ah batteries in 3 hours
  • Compatible with all PACKOUT™ products
  • Compatible with all M18™ batteries
  • Cord management and removable cord
  • Batteries latch to charger
  • Charges 2 batteries simultaneously at rapid charge rate
