Product Highlights

The GMP 2-15 Professional Material moisture meter with integrated thermo-hygrometer is your go-to tool for fast and accurate moisture readings. It measures moisture in 37 types of wood and 10 types of building materials, displaying results on a high-contrast, color LCD screen. The integrated thermo-hygrometer sensor monitors ambient temperature and relative humidity to always give you comprehensive data. The adjustable threshold scale simplifies the interpretation of results, making it easier for you to make accurate evaluations. Its robust design, rubber-enforced housing and IP65 rating can withstand tough jobsite conditions - giving you a reliable performance no matter the job.

Equipment & Application

This Material moisture meter is essential for drywallers, bricklayers, carpenters, painters, facility managers, and architects. It helps identify moisture sources, assess material moisture, and ensure optimal conditions for painting and construction. Whether you're checking wood moisture before installation or evaluating humidity levels in a building, the GMP 2-15 Professional supports your work with outstanding precision. Also, its compatibility with the Li-ion battery pack gives you lasting power, so you can focus on your tasks without interruptions.

Additional Information

The GMP 2-15 Professional features an automatic self-test before each use to ensure accuracy and reliability. And if you want more confidence in its performance, it also offers the option to perform a manual self-test. The center button allows you to hold measurements, making it easier to record and analyze data. With its durable design and advanced features, this moisture meter is always ready to help you achieve the best results in your projects.
