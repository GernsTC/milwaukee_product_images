User benefit

  • 11 metal drill bits Ø 1.5-6.5 mm with titanium nitride coating that excel through their fast drilling progress and particularly long service life (not suitable for use in aluminium).
  • 4 robust carbide-tipped masonry drill bits Ø 4-7 mm that impress with their long service life, fast stock removal, low heat development and precision.
  • 4 sharp wood drill bits Ø 4-8 mm with centring tip and shoulder cutter for precise, tear-free hole drilling.
  • 10 extra-hard screwdriver bits for use with the most commonly used screws with Phillips, Pozidriv, Torx and longitudinal slot heads. Comes with universal magnetic holder.
  • Suitable for all power tool brands.
