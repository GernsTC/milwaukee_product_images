Features

  • Delivers up to 6x faster charging to 80% vs. standard charger for more productivity
  • 15 minute super charge on the M18™REDLITHIUM™ FORGE™ 6.0 Ah to 80%
  • Adaptive charge distribution delivers adaptive simultaneous charging for multiple batteries to maximise productivity
  • REDLINK™ intelligence, charger communicates with the battery pack to ensure optimised charge speed and performance
  • M18™ batteries ≤ 5.0 Ah are charged simultaneously with the same charge rate, M18™ batteries ≥ 5.0 Ah are charged simultaneously, but the first pack attached has priority and gets charged with a higher charge rate
  • Compatible with all M18™ batteries
  • Mounting on back allows for versatility of orientations
  • COOL-CYCLE™, active cooling system provides high speed cooling for less downtime. Active cooling feature works with batteries that have COOL-CYCLE™ capability
