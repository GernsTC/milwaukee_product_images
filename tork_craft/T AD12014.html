Magnetic Impact Wrench / Ratchet Adaptor

Length: 60mm

A Magnetic Impact Wrench / Ratchet Adaptor is a specialized tool designed to connect a 1/4" drive ratchet socket to a 1/2" drive impact wrench. This adapter offers a convenient way to utilize the powerful torque of an impact wrench for precise ratcheting tasks. This is useful for tasks that require precise torque control, such as working on delicate components or when you need to avoid over-tightening fasteners.

Application:

  • Used to convert the 1/2" drive of an impact wrench or ratchet to a 1/4" hex drive that fits different accessories.
