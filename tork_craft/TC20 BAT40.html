Tork Craft | Battery Pack 20v 4.0ah Li-ion

TC20 BAT40

Battery pack 20V 4.0Ah Li-Ion

The battery pack fits all 20V Tork Craft Power Tools, is lightweight Lithium-Ion, and has a 4.0 Ah electric charge.

The battery pack is fitted with LED battery capacity indicator lights and also has vents for cooling.

Recharge only with the Tork Craft TC20VABC-01 and TC20VABC-02 chargers.
