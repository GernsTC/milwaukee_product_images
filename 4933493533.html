Features

  • World's first integrated dust extractor drilling hammer
  • The highly efficient HEPA filter collects 99.97% of airborne particles down to 0.3 microns
  • AUTOSTOP™ shuts down the tool to protect the user of sharp movement in bind up situations
  • Optimised for overhead applications
  • AUTOPULSE™ on-board automatic filter cleaning mechanism for an on-tool dust extractor, which increases the air flow efficiency as well as the filter life time
  • Anti-vibration system (AVS) for decreased user fatigue and lower vibration exposure. Also, an added anti-vibration side handle for lowering the vibration exposure even further
  • Translucent dust box allows user to see the current level of dust inside as well as when the dust box is full
  • ONE-KEY™ tool tracking & security offers a free of charge cloud-based tracking network and inventory management platform for your tools. ONE-KEY™ also features a remote locking functionality
  • The DNA of our FUEL™ platform redefines the balance of cordless technologies. MILWAUKEE®'s POWERSTATE™ brushless motor, REDLITHIUM™ battery pack and REDLINK PLUS™ electronic intelligence delivering outstanding power, run time and durability
  • Flexible dust shroud increasing the user's comfort and efficiency, especially when drilling overhead between pipes and tight spaces
  • Flexible battery system: works with all MILWAUKEE® M18™ batteries
