Features

  • The tool allows user to quickly cut thick wall steel pipes up to 1″/ 33.7 mm
  • Up to 4x faster cuts vs traditional cutting tools like bandsaws or angle grinders
  • Provides up to 50 consistently straight cuts on one charge (M18 B5)​
  • The foldable pipe rail allows for one hand operation and ensures perfectly straight cuts minimising deburring and other rework needs
  • The tool allows for safe cuts without the need to clamp the pipe providing users with maximum flexibility and safety​
  • The plunging mechanism helps to prevent overcutting into other objects around application​
  • Light weight design allows for all day use (Only 3.7 kg with battery)​
  • Carbide tipped blade ensures long durability and clean cuts ready for threading on carbon steel and galvanized steel pipes
  • Design allows for in place and on ground cutting applications​
  • The DNA of our FUEL™ platform redefines the balance of cordless technologies. MILWAUKEE®'s POWERSTATE™ brushless motor, REDLITHIUM™ battery pack and REDLINK PLUS™ electronic intelligence delivering outstanding power, run time and durability
  • Flexible battery system: works with all MILWAUKEE® M18™ batteries
