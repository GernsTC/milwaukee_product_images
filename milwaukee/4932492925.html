Features

  • High performance threader dies ensure perfect threads and long lasting performance in combination with the MILWAUKEE® threaders
  • Dies are available in two types of thread geometries: BSPT (British Standard Pipe Taper) and NPT (National Taper Pipe)
  • Dies are available in two diferent grades: Alloy dies for long lasting performance on black and galvanised pipes and HS dies for best performance on stainless steel pipes
  • One set contains 4 single cutting dies that are needed to equip one die head
