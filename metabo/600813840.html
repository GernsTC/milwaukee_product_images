Product Description

  • Metabo's strongest SDS-plus cordless hammer for quick drilling and efficient chiseling in concrete and masonry
  • Maximum drill performance, efficiency and service life thanks to brushless technology and high-performance hammer action
  • Combination hammer with 3 functions: hammer drilling, drilling and chiselling
  • Metabo VibraTech (MVT): integrated damping system to reduce vibrations in order to protect the user's health
  • Perfect ergonomics thanks to the centre of gravity being near the hand and optimised handle contour
  • Metabo Quick: quick change between SDS Plus hammer chuck and keyless chuck for many different applications
  • Can be switched to anticlockwise rotation to easily loosen tilted tool attachments
  • Optional reduction of speed and impact energy for starting a drill hole or working in softer materials
  • Restart protection: prevents unintentional start-up after battery pack change
  • Double safety when the drill bit jams: No kick-back thanks to the mechanic decoupling plus ultra-fast sensor-controlled shutdown
  • LED work light for optimum view of the drilling site
  • Many brands, one battery pack system: This product can be combined with all 18V battery packs and chargers of the CAS brands: www.cordless-alliance-systems.com
