Product Description

  • Robust die cast aluminium gear housing for optimum heat dissipation and durability
  • Rotating carbon brush bridge for maximum performance even in anti-clockwise operation, e.g. for the removal of stubborn screws
  • Vario (V)-Electronics for working at customised speeds to suit various application materials
  • Metabo S-automatic safety clutch: mechanical decoupling of the drive for safe working should the drill stop unexpectedly
