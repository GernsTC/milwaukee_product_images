Features

  • The M18 FUEL™ rivet tool with ONE-KEY™ is an optimised cordless solution that delivers fast, easy riveting while maintaining performance, durability, and size
  • Pulls up to 900 rivets with a rivet size of 4.8 mm in aluminium or 500 rivets with a size of 6.4 mm in aluminium on a fully charged M18™ 2.0 Ah battery pack
  • Fasten all blind rivets including aluminium, steel, stainless steel and copper from 4.8 mm, 6.0 mm, 6.4 mm and 7.0 mm to give you a bigger range of applications to be completed
  • 20,000 N of pulling force with a stroke length of 30 mm gives you the power to complete the larger diameters effortlessly
  • The premium steel alloy 3-jaw design delivers more work between jaw changes
  • Tool-free access to easily clean jaws. On-board nose piece storage and wrench for user convenience
  • The transparent mandrel collector allows you to see when the mandrel collector is full, avoiding any mandrel jammings and minimising down time to unblock jammings also avoiding costly repairs at the same time
  • ONE-KEY™ tool tracking & security offers a free of charge cloud-based tracking network and inventory management platform for your tools. ONE-KEY™ also features a remote locking functionality
  • The DNA of our FUEL™ platform redefines the balance of cordless technologies. MILWAUKEE®'s POWERSTATE™ brushless motor, REDLITHIUM™ battery pack and REDLINK PLUS™ electronic intelligence delivering  outstanding power, run time and durability
  • Flexible battery system: works with all MILWAUKEE® M18™ batteries
