Features

  • Designed for users working out of gang boxes or other steel storage spaces on job sites
  • Tool free mounting in multiple orientations via strong magnetic base or cleat, maximising storage space
  • Illuminate stored supplies with built-in 450 lm flood light
  • Simultaneous charging of two batteries at rapid charge rate for both M18™ and M12™, then automatically switching to the next two
  • Simultaneous charging of personal devices with the two USB-C 15 W ports
  • Charger features two M18™ only bays and two M18™ and M12™ hybrid bays
  • Fully compatible with all M18™ and M12™ batteries
  • Two integrated levers for quick removal from metal surfaces
